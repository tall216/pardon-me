## Frequently asked

**How do I trigger a call?**
Open the app and use the trigger control, or schedule a call ahead of time.
On iPhone, the volume-button trigger works while the app is in the foreground.

**Does it use my real phone service?**
No. Pardon Me never places, receives, or interferes with real calls.

**Does it collect my data?**
No. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing leaves your phone.
See our [Privacy Policy](./privacy).
