# Rules of the show

Read in full before planning any episode. Almost everything is `OPEN`; this
file lists the decisions so they can be made.

## Confirmed

- Alderinos is drawn in the Cartoony style through the Cartoony pipeline.
  Character sheets govern visuals. These notes govern story.
- Setting: Alderinos Pub & Pasta, Collinwood, Cleveland, 1999.

## Format (`OPEN`)

- Episode length.
- Structure: cold open? A and B plots? Return to status quo at the end?
- Does the show ever leave the pub, and how often?

## Tone (`OPEN`)

- Sitcom warm, sitcom mean, or something stranger?
- Who is the audience?
- How 1999 is it: period piece, or just the vibe?

## Cartoony physics (`OPEN`)

What the story logic allows. To decide:

- Squash and stretch as real forces, or just a drawing style?
- Can characters get hurt? For how long?
- Fourth wall: never, signs only, or anyone?
- Anything supernatural, or strictly a pub?

## Continuity (`OPEN`)

- Do characters age? Does the calendar move toward Y2K?
- Which landmarks are fixed?
- Default outfit as silhouette; alternates per character, listed in their file.
- Facts stated on screen go in `continuity-log.md` and stay true.
