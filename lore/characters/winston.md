## History

`PROPOSED`

- Grew up in a city (unnamed; `OPEN` which one) with his mother, Celeste, who
  is Uncle D's older sister.
- Sent to Quail Hollow at the start of the show because Celeste's work moved
  her somewhere a nine-year-old could not follow for a while. This is treated
  as ordinary and temporary. (See `OPEN` question in `_cast.md`. Nothing on
  screen should suggest anything sadder than that.)
- Had never met Uncle D in person before the first episode. Had heard
  eleven-minute phone calls about him for his whole life and arrived with a
  binder.
- Has never crossed a creek on a log, climbed a water tower, or eaten pie for
  breakfast. All three happen in season one.

## What he wants

`PROPOSED`

- **Surface want:** for things to go according to plan. He has a plan for
  everything, including for Uncle D not having a plan.
- **Deep want:** to be useful to the people he loves in a way they can see.
  He believes being right is how you show up for people. The show slowly
  teaches him it is one way.
- **Fear:** that he is in the way. He is not, and is the last to know.

## How he operates

`PROPOSED`

- Lists. On a clipboard he can produce from behind his back. Numbered,
  lettered, cross-referenced.
- Keeps **the ledger**: every favor Uncle D is owed by the town. Reads the
  total aloud once a season. It is absurd and growing.
- Asks direct questions because he has learned Uncle D will answer them.
- Is braver than he looks and does not know it. Will follow Uncle D onto the
  log while explaining why the bridge is safer, every step.
- Is kind to animals, formal with adults, and bewildered by June.

## Relationships

`PROPOSED`

- **Uncle D:** his hero, his project, his responsibility. He would never say
  any of that. He calls him "Uncle D" always; the day he says just "D" will be
  an episode.
- **June:** first and best friend. She does everything he would not and he
  documents it.
- **Mabel:** the adult he trusts most to be sensible. He goes to her when a
  plan needs a grown-up and D is the problem.
- **Mayor Pinkney:** the only person in town who reads Winston's written
  complaints, and the only person who files them.
- **The Covey:** he is the only person the quail do not scatter from. He has
  theories about why. The show never confirms any of them.

## Lines he would say

`PROPOSED`

- "I have a list."
- "Technically, that's not a plan. That's a sentence."
- "Uncle D, I'm writing this down."
- "The bridge is right there."
- "Why are the quail looking at me?"

## Lines he would never say

- Anything that makes fun of Uncle D in front of the town. He saves it for the
  porch.
- "I don't know." He says "I haven't found out yet."
- Anything cruel. He is not capable of it, which is the point.
