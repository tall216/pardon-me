# Mayor Horace Pinkney

`PROPOSED` throughout. Regular supporting cast. The closest thing the show has
to an antagonist, which is to say: not one.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Age | Sixties, round, mustached, sashed. Wears the sash to the creek. |
| Role | Mayor of Quail Hollow for as long as anyone recalls. Unopposed, mostly because nobody else wants the desk. |
| Voice | Booming, formal, loves a proclamation. Mispronounces one word per speech and will not be corrected. |

## History

- Elected after promising a second road out of the valley. There is still one
  road. He has a plan for the second one. It is the same plan every year.
- Believes Quail Hollow is one ribbon-cutting away from being "on the map."
  Owns the ribbon. Owns the scissors. Has cut the ribbon on a bench.
- Considers Uncle D a threat to civic order and also his only real friend,
  though he would call it "a working relationship."

## Wants

- **Surface:** ceremony. Anything that can be opened, dedicated, or named.
- **Deep:** to be remembered. He is already the only mayor anyone remembers,
  and it is not enough.

## How he operates

- Files everything. Winston's complaints get a stamp and a folder.
- Over-promises in public, under-delivers in private, is forgiven by lunch.
- His office desk is the size of a boat and he is always behind it, which
  means we rarely see his legs, which means the one time we do is a joke.

## Outfits

| Outfit | Description | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default** | Three-piece suit, sash, a hat with a feather that the Covey resents. | Always. |
| **Emergency** | The suit with the jacket off and the sash on over the vest. | Any crisis. He considers this "sleeves rolled up." |

## Lines

- "By the power vested in me, which is considerable—"
- "Mr. Dupree. What have you done to my town."
- "Young man, that complaint is *filed*."
