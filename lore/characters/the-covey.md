# The Covey

`PROPOSED` throughout. Not a character. A presence.

## What it is

The flock of quail that lives on every fence rail in Quail Hollow. Roughly
twelve birds, drawn as one unit, moving as one unit. They do not talk. They
understand everything. They are the show's laugh track, chorus, and conscience.

## Rules

- Always visible in a wide shot of town, somewhere, watching.
- **Scatter** at the exact beat a plan fails. Same formation every time (the
  pipeline should treat it as a reusable cycle). Return to the rail within
  the scene.
- Never scatter from Winston. He is the only person they stay still for. The
  show never explains this. Characters may notice it; nobody may solve it.
- Never harmed, never caught, never cooked. The one time someone proposes
  quail for dinner, the Covey is at the window.
- May be used as a visual "cut to reaction" instead of a human reaction shot.

## The water tower quail

The painted quail on the tower is a separate running gag. It was painted by
Uncle D, was supposed to look proud, looks worried, and looks slightly more
worried each time it is shown. The Covey appears to be aware of it.

## For the Director

Use the Covey for timing. If a joke needs a beat, give it to the birds. If an
episode has not used them by the midpoint, the town does not feel like the
Hollow.
