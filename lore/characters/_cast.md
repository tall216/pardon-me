# Cast at a glance

One line each. Full files exist for the leads and the regular supporting cast.
Characters without a file are walk-ons and may be renamed or dropped.

## Leads

- **Uncle D** (`CONFIRMED` exists; details `PROPOSED`) — the uncle. Easygoing,
  improvising, beloved, wrong about half the time and never worried about which
  half. File: `uncle-d.md`.
- **Winston** (`CONFIRMED` exists; details `PROPOSED`) — the nephew, nine,
  serious, organized, newly arrived. File: `winston.md`.

## Regular supporting cast (`PROPOSED`)

- **Mabel Okafor** — runs Mabel's Place. Uncle D's oldest friend and sharpest
  critic. File: `mabel.md`.
- **Mayor Horace Pinkney** — the mayor. Pompous, harmless, convinced the town
  is one ribbon-cutting away from greatness. File: `mayor-pinkney.md`.
- **June Delacroix** — Winston's first friend in town, ten, fearless, always
  slightly muddy. File: `june.md`.
- **The Covey** — the flock of quail. Not a character, a presence. File:
  `the-covey.md`.

## Walk-ons (`PROPOSED`, no files yet)

- **The Librarian** — unnamed, whispers even outdoors.
- **Deacon Pruitt** — fixes what Uncle D breaks, slowly, for pie.
- **The Delivery Driver** — only ever seen from the Top, never makes it down.

## Running gags (use at most two per episode)

`PROPOSED`

1. **"Pardon me."** Uncle D says it just before doing something nobody should
   do. The town has learned to duck when they hear it.
2. **The Covey scatters** at the exact beat a plan fails, always from a fence
   rail, always in the same formation.
3. **Winston's ledger.** He writes down what Uncle D is owed. The number is
   read aloud once a season and is absurd.
4. **The log vs. the bridge.** Any crossing of Covey Creek.
5. **The one phone signal** at the Top, never where anyone is standing.
6. **The water tower quail** looks more worried each time it is shown.
