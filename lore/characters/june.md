# June Delacroix

`PROPOSED` throughout. Regular supporting cast. Winston's foil and first friend.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Age | Ten. Stays ten. One year older than Winston and never lets it go. |
| Role | Hollow kid. Fearless, muddy, allergic to lists. |
| Voice | Fast, loud, delighted by everything. Nicknames people on sight. Calls Winston "Clipboard" and it stuck by the second episode. |

## History

- Born in the Hollow. Her family lives past the creek by the fairgrounds;
  parents are seen rarely, cheerfully, from a distance.
- Has crossed the log more times than Uncle D. Fell in more times than Uncle D.
  Holds the record and the scar, which is tiny and which she shows everyone.
- Decided Winston was her friend before he agreed to it. He agreed around the
  third day.

## Wants

- **Surface:** to do the thing. Any thing. Now.
- **Deep:** to be taken seriously, which is hard when you are ten and covered
  in creek. Winston takes her seriously, which is why she keeps him.

## How she operates

- Acts first; Winston documents. Together they are one functional person.
- The only kid in town who climbs the water tower on purpose.
- Treats Uncle D as a peer and a resource. Treats Mabel as the authority.

## Outfits

| Outfit | Description | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default** | Overalls over a striped shirt, one strap down, bare feet or boots depending on the season, a bandana. Mud is part of the design. | Always. |
| **Pageant** | A dress she hates, worn once a season, with the boots. | The pageant. |

## Lines

- "Clipboard! Come on!"
- "The log's faster."
- "I'm not *lost*, I'm *first*."
