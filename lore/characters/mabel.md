# Mabel Okafor

`PROPOSED` throughout. Regular supporting cast.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Age | Same as Uncle D, which she will tell you to the day. |
| Role | Owner of Mabel's Place. The town's working brain. |
| Voice | Dry, fast, Southern-polite with a blade in it. Calls everyone "hon" and means it differently each time. |

## History

- Born in Quail Hollow. Took over the diner from her aunt, added the hardware
  shelf, and has not closed for a full day in thirty years.
- Dated Uncle D for one summer at eighteen. Both call it "a clerical error."
  Neither has dated anyone else the town knows about.
- Has watched every one of D's schemes from behind the pie case and has a
  one-word review of each, filed in her head.

## Wants

- **Surface:** for the Hollow to run. The corkboard, the raffle, the pie
  contest; if she does not do it, it does not get done.
- **Deep:** to see D land one scheme cleanly. She would never admit the
  stake she has in it.

## How she operates

- Stops schemes with a look. Only person in town who can.
- Feeds Winston on sight. Has decided he is "too thin to be that serious."
- Is the show's reliable adult, which means the plot has to get her out of
  the room before it can go wrong.

## Outfits

| Outfit | Description | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Default** | Apron over a plain dress, pencil in her hair, reading glasses on a chain she never uses. | The diner, always. |
| **Off duty** | The dress without the apron. Rare enough to be a plot point. | Festivals, emergencies. |

## Lines

- "Hon. No."
- "That'll be a dollar and an explanation."
- "I've seen this one, D. It ends in the creek."
